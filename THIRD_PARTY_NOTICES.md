## Fonts

No Moonstone font files are bundled in the IPK. Enact prefers the TV's installed LG Smart UI and Moonstone fonts through CSS `local()` sources. The Miso and Museo Sans font files supplied with `@enact/moonstone` are removed from the package after the Enact build.
